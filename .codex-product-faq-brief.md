# Product FAQ Separate Section

## Objective
Move product-specific FAQs out of the narrow `Know Your Setting` product-info column and render them as a clearly separated, centered section on the product details page.

## Aesthetic direction
Quiet luxury and editorial clarity using the existing House of Diams navy/pale-blue system. The FAQ should read as an intentional page section, not another configurator accordion.

## Structure
- Keep description, details, shipping, and care inside the existing ProductTabs area.
- Remove Product FAQs from that ProductTabs instance on product details pages.
- Add a standalone Product FAQ section after the main product-detail section and before Related Products.
- Section outer shell spans the page width visually; FAQ content is centered and capped around 850-960px.
- Center the section eyebrow/title/introduction, while question and answer copy remains left aligned for readability.
- Use accessible accordion buttons with clear dividers, generous vertical rhythm, and chevrons.
- Do not render the section when no valid FAQs exist.
- Maintain responsive spacing and touch-friendly controls.

## Typography and colors
Use existing project fonts, `#0A1628` brand navy, white and navy-derived pale-blue surfaces. No peach/beige.

## Constraints
- Existing Next.js/Tailwind application at `D:\Main Hod\house-of-diams`.
- Preserve data flow and product FAQ content.
- Avoid changing the general `/faq` page or unrelated ProductTabs consumers.
- Prefer a focused reusable component under `components/product`.

## Output path
Implement directly in `D:\Main Hod\house-of-diams`.

## Image needs
None.
